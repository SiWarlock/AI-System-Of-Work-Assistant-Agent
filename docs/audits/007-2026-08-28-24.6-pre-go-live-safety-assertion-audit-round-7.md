## Findings: 23 candidates → **0 confirmed**

Every candidate went to an independent verifier told to default to refuted. All were knocked
down, most commonly by one of:

- the value comes from a **validated projection**, not a literal — derived, not asserted;
- the file's own header **documents the posture** (fail-closed, reachability-waivered,
  deliberately inert);
- the constant carries **no safety claim** (a port number, a version string, a UI label);
- the path is **dormant or owner-gated** and says so.

⛔ **This is not a clean bill for the codebase.** It is a clean bill for *this surface* on *this
question*. The same session found, by hand and by other sweeps: `sow-doctor` unrunnable
(`24.143`), a false `single_owner_lock` finding (`24.139`), a locked keychain reported as
"missing" (`§ARM-17`), the gbrain version pin unable to pass (`24.142`), and `db_unavailable`
minting `worker_down` for a month.

⭐ **What the null result does support:** round 4's headline — *the project reliably CATCHES
this defect class; what it fails at is PROPAGATING the catch.* The archetypes this audit was
created for (the chrome "Egress: local-only" pill, the absent-notice-implies-safety shape) were
found, fixed, and have not recurred on the surfaces most likely to reproduce them.
