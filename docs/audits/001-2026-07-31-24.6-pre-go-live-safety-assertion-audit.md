### F1 · `retrieveLocalEmbed`'s rule-5 egress guard is OPTIONAL at the seam where it will matter
**Source:** AC-2 · **Reported severity:** high · ⚠ **RE-GRADED by the orchestrator after resolving AC-2's cross-partition escalation — see below** · **Safety rule 5** (Employer-Work egress veto)

`packages/knowledge/src/gbrain/local-embed.ts:214-240` — `retrieveLocalEmbed`'s egress floor derives from `backend.egressClass` (`:64-67`), a field the caller **self-declares** on the injected `EmbeddingBackend`. The mechanism that would *prove* it — `egressGate?: RetrievalEgressGate` (`:107`, the reuse of the real endpoint-proof `egressVeto`) — **is optional** (`:244` `if (egressGate !== undefined)`).

⭐ **AC-2 escalated the decisive question across its own partition boundary** (*"does `apps/worker` bind a real gate in production?"*), correctly, since composition is outside its surface. **The orchestrator resolved it. Measured, repo-wide, src and test:**
- **`retrieveLocalEmbed` has ZERO production callers.** Every reference outside its own definition is the eval harness (`packages/evals/src/retrieval/{recall,corpus}.ts`) or a lesson citation.
- **`egressGate` is bound in TESTS ONLY** — `packages/knowledge/test/gbrain-local-embed.test.ts:215,232,244` (deny/throw/allow gates).
- **The only `EmbeddingBackend` construction is `packages/evals/src/retrieval/corpus.ts:53`**, described in-source as *"a recorded, zero-egress EmbeddingBackend… pure in-memory."*

⇒ **RE-GRADE: this is NOT a live rule-5 breach — it is a live rule-5 ARMING PRECONDITION WITH NO ENFORCEMENT MECHANISM.** Nothing reaches the unproven path today because nothing reaches the function at all. ⛔ **But under the threat-model ruling that is exactly the wrong reason to close it:** the module's own comment (`:229`) already states a remote backend *"MUST bind `egressGate`"* at 13.17/13.8c wiring — **and "MUST" is prose, not a gate.** An optional guard is type-indistinguishable from a bound one at the call site, so the arming slice that wires real retrieval can satisfy the compiler while omitting the proof (contracts **L106**'s shape aimed at a guard rather than a signal).
⇒ **ACTION: a tracked task, required before real (non-recorded) retrieval is wired** — make the gate non-optional for any backend not proven local, or make the unproven path unrepresentable. ⚠ **Do NOT close this by reading `:229`'s comment as coverage** — that is the asserted-constant mechanism in a comment.
