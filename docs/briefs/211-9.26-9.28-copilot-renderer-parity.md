## Why these two bundle

Same surface, same file, same review context, one commit's worth of reasoning: both are about the renderer
treating a Copilot answer as candidate data rather than trusted output. Neither changes a safety *decision* — the
server-side gate stays load-bearing by design. **Neither is a live defect**, which is why they bundle safely:
9.26 is symmetry with four sibling read paths, 9.28 is a latent consumer-side gap. If either turns out to be
live during the slice, **split it out and say so** — a live rule-5 finding gets its own commit.

## ⚠ Scope guard — do NOT change what the notice MEANS

The owner-chosen posture stands and is **out of scope**: absence of a notice on a *non-Employer-Work cloud*
answer is deliberate, and `notice_is_scope_blind_at_the_renderer` pins that the renderer cannot see workspace
type. **Do not "fix" case 2 with renderer-side suppression** — that would reverse an owner decision *and* migrate
a rule-5 decision out of the worker. You are making the disclosure impossible to *drop*, not changing *when* it
applies.
