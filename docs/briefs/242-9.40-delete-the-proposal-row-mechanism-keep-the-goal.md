proposal-row affordance — a renderer-local optional label, a permanently-disabled
button, and the one test that renders the branch — because no producer can supply the

Approvals*) survives as a **separate tracked task** naming the corrected producer
shape. **This unblocks desktop.**

**LEAD RULING (autonomous mode, recorded in `docs/team-handoffs/020-…`).** The

genuine product commitment**, and that is the half a reader will get wrong.

## ⭐ PREMISES — ALL MEASURED FROM SOURCE, and two are stronger than the ruling stated
**Verified by the orchestrator before briefing, per the lead's instruction. Re-check
any that look wrong; a premise correction at Step 2.5 is a success, not friction.**

1. **`UiSafeCopilotAnswer` structurally cannot carry an action — CONFIRMED, and the
   in-code statement is more explicit than the ruling quoted.**
   `packages/contracts/src/api/ui-safe.ts` — the interface is exactly `answer` +
   `citations` + `egressProcessor?`, and its header states: *"NO side effects: if the
   answer implies an action, that becomes a ProposedAction routed to Approvals —
   **never carried on this shape**."* ⇒ **option A as the tracker phrases it
   (*"`finish` populates `proposalLabel`"*) cannot be built without contradicting the
   answer seam's own stated design.** That is why the mechanism goes rather than
   getting a producer.
2. **What is being deleted is ONE doc comment on a renderer-local optional field —
   CONFIRMED.** `apps/desktop/renderer/surfaces/copilot/Copilot.tsx:75`
   (`readonly proposalLabel?: string;`), rendered at `:189-191`. **Not a frozen
   contract entry, not a schema, not an Appendix-A row** ⇒ no cross-doc invariant, no
   snapshot, no seam round. Weigh the deletion accordingly.
3. ⭐ **UPGRADED FROM INFERENCE TO MEASUREMENT — and it produces a better argument than
   "it's dead."** The ruling flagged the disabled-button point as *inference*. Measured:
   the button carries a **hardcoded `disabled`** (no condition) and **no `onClick`
   handler at all** — navigation is absent by construction, not by state. ⭐ **And its
   own comment reveals it was a deliberate placeholder:** *"The Approvals surface +
   navigation land with that page; the affordance is honest-disabled."* ⇒ **its stated
   precondition HAS SINCE LANDED — the Approvals surface exists — and the affordance STILL
   cannot be wired**, because `proposalLabel?: string` carries **no approval id**, and a
   *"Review in Approvals"* control needs an id to navigate to. **That is the load-bearing
   argument: not "nobody built the producer," but "the shape is wrong for any producer
   that could exist."** State it that way in the commit.
4. **`CopilotTurn`'s export — the FUNCTION stays; the EXPORT is the open question.**
   The function is used in production at `Copilot.tsx:375` (`<CopilotTurn … />`) — **but
   that is a same-file use and does not require the `export` keyword.** The export's
   only external consumer is `test-dom/copilot-panel.test.tsx:7`, which imports it
   specifically to render the branch being deleted (its own comment at `:118` says so).
   ⇒ **after the deletion the export may be unnecessary — establish it, do not assume
   either way** (see Step-2.5 Q2).
5. ⭐ **GOOD NEWS the ruling's warning did not have: the 9.24/9.28 assertions are NOT
   in the deletion path.** The lead correctly warned the deletion must not silently drop
   the share-blocking rule-5 assertions. Measured: the block to delete is **one `it(...)`
   at `test-dom/copilot-panel.test.tsx:119-140`**; the 9.24 blocks are separate
   `describe`s (`:159`, `:275`), 9.28 (`:408`, `:433`) and 9.34 (`:474`) likewise. ⇒ **the
   risk is LOW — but the check is still required**, because of (6).
6. ⛔ **A STALE COMMENT IN THE TEST THAT SURVIVES — the easy thing to miss.** The *next*
   test (`:141`, *"a turn with no citations and no proposal renders a bare answer"*)
   justifies itself with *"'no proposal' is now structurally guaranteed (`finish` never
   sets `proposalLabel`), not a fixture choice."* **After the field is gone that sentence
   is meaningless** — it cites a field that no longer exists as the reason for a still-
   valid test. **Update it; do not delete the test.** (This is contracts L94: a change
   must land in every channel that carries the claim, including the ones that merely
   repeat it.)
