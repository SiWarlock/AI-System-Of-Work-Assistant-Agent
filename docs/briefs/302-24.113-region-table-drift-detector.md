1. ⛔ **WHICH SURFACE BINDS — ajv (emitted JSON Schema) OR THE ZOD `_def` GRAPH, OR BOTH?** The docblock ran both *"because either alone is half an answer — ajv and Zod are separate implementations of the same constraint and could disagree."* ⭐ **That is exactly `029`'s concordance rule satisfied properly: two methods count as independent only when they COULD have disagreed, and these genuinely could.**
