## Things to flag at Step 2.5

1. ⛔⛔ **DOES `URL_CREDENTIAL_PARAM` COME ACROSS — AND WHAT HAPPENS TO ITS NEGATIVE LOOKAHEAD UNDER `stripMarkers`? THIS IS THE LOAD-BEARING QUESTION AND MY REASONING IS A HYPOTHESIS TO CHECK, NOT AN INSTRUCTION.**
   **The premise, checkable at source:** integrations' `URL_CREDENTIAL_PARAM` ends `=(?!\[REDACTED\])[^&#\s]+` — the lookahead exists so an ALREADY-SCRUBBED value passes and a scrubbed URL is not over-dropped. ⛔ **But `@sow/domain`'s `looksUnsafe` applies `stripMarkers` BEFORE testing**, and `REDACTED_CREDENTIAL` is `"[REDACTED:credential]"` (`packages/contracts/src/observability/log-record.ts:29`). ⇒ ***`?key=[REDACTED:credential]` becomes `?key=^` before the net ever sees it, the lookahead no longer suppresses, and the net FIRES on already-redacted content — an over-refusal integrations does not have, because integrations does not strip.***
   ⭐ **That is `### 24.118`'s OWN RULE firing on its own remedy: check PATTERNS *and* COMPOSITION.** ⚠ **I reasoned this from source and RAN NOTHING. Falsify it first.**
   **My default vote: promote `GOOGLE_API_KEY` (+ its scrub alternative) THIS SLICE; HOLD `URL_CREDENTIAL_PARAM` and route the lookahead/stripping interaction back to me.** Rationale: the Google detector is a pure capability addition with no composition interaction; the URL-param net is a behaviour change whose direction I have reasoned and not measured, and this round has already paid twice for shipping a "plausibly desirable" on a rule-7 surface.
2. **DOES ADDING A NET RED `### 24.120`'s MARKER-FILLER PROPERTY GUARD?** The guard derives its cases from `CREDENTIAL_NETS` by reflection, so a new net brings new cases automatically — **by design, and untested against these particular nets.** `URL_CREDENTIAL_PARAM`'s `[^&#\s]+` **excludes whitespace**, which is the *identical* structural property that made `URL_USERINFO_CREDENTIAL` the one net `### 24.120` had to fix. **Default vote: run the guard FIRST, report before/after, and treat a red as a FINDING to route — not as something to work around.** ⛔ **Do not adjust the guard to make a net fit.**
3. **IS `GOOGLE_API_KEY` A DETECTION NET, A SCRUB TOKEN, OR BOTH?** integrations has it in both. **Default: both.** A detector with no matching scrub alternative means `redactString` cannot clear the value and the whole field drops — a strictly worse availability outcome on a path where availability is already a live finding (`### 24.123`).
4. **HOW IS THE TIGHTENING PRICED?** Adding a net can only ADD refusals. **Default: measure the delta over one stated corpus with ONE denominator, report it as a count over that denominator, and name the corpus's relationship to real content.** ⛔ **Do not report a percentage on a small bucket** (the `0 of 6` ruling; `### 24.123` records a point estimate drawn from `6 of 25` as a defect). ⚠ **If the delta is non-trivial, that is `### 24.123`'s to price — route it, do not absorb it.**
