`livingVault`'s empty-array identity, **propose has no natural "nothing happened" success** — a
mint either happens or does not. So the new activity's **unarmed-identity branch** needs a value.

- **Worker's position (recorded WITH its reasoning so the next Step-2.5 starts from a position
