## The design fork — NAMED, not pre-ruled, EXCEPT for one option that IS ruled out

**Option A — STRUCTURAL:** make the unsound state unrepresentable — a foreign-workspace note **cannot** land unprefixed, because the path is derived from / validated against the bound `workspaceId` at commit. ⭐ This is the project's own preferred shape ([L87](../../packages/contracts/LESSONS.md#87)'s nominal brand, [L103](../../packages/contracts/LESSONS.md#103)'s make-it-unrepresentable).
**Option B — DETECTOR:** a scan that fires the moment content belonging to a non-assigned workspace appears unprefixed, surfacing a durable, queryable signal.

⛔ **RULED OUT — DO NOT PROPOSE IT, AND THE REASONING IS PINNED HERE SO IT IS NOT RE-PROPOSED: a typed opt-in flag at the config site** (e.g. requiring `legacyBridgeAcknowledged: true`) **is NEITHER structural enforcement NOR a detector — it is a better comment.** It relocates the operator guard from prose into a type without deriving anything or detecting anything, and **the owner's condition explicitly says an operator guard in a comment does not discharge this.** Moving it into a flag does not change what it is.

**Choose A or B (or both) at Step 2.5 with the code in front of you, and say why.** ⭐ **My lean is A** — a detector tells you after the fact, and the threat model here is *"the gates are open."* ⚠ **But A's cost is real and (U3) is where it bites: if the legitimate-unprefixed case cannot be expressed cleanly, a detector that fires reliably beats a structural rule that has to be widened until it stops meaning anything.** **B is an honest answer if you can show A degrades that way.**

## ⚠ The layering question you must surface, not resolve silently

Option A couples the **writer** (knowledge, rule-1 territory) to a fact about the **Copilot legacy policy** (a serving concern). ⛔ **A writer that imports a serving policy is a layering inversion.** ⇒ **the invariant must be expressed in the writer's OWN terms** — *"a note's path must be consistent with its bound workspace"* — with the legacy exception expressed as data (which workspace may be unprefixed), **not as an import.** **If you cannot do that cleanly, that is a Step-2.5 finding and I will re-rule rather than have you build the inversion.**
