## Acceptance criteria (what "done" means)
- [ ] The suite loads the 23-entry meeting-closeout corpus via `src/harness/corpus-loader.ts` and asserts the corpus floor (≥ 20).
- [ ] For every corpus entry labeled **invented/unstated owner or date**, the oracle **REJECTS** (unstated ⇒ `TBD`/clarification; never a fabricated value) — REQ-F-017.
- [ ] For every entry labeled **evidence-grounded**, the oracle **PASSES** (an explicitly-stated owner/date is preserved, not forced to `TBD`).
- [ ] Scoring is a **hard 100% floor** — no-inference is a safety invariant, not a soft recall bar; a single fabricated-value pass FAILS the suite.
- [ ] The suite is **read-only over the untrusted transcript** (ING-7 posture) and provider-free (deterministic — no live model call).
- [ ] The suite runs green; `/preflight` clean.
