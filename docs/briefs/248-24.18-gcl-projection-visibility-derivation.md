# /tdd brief — gcl_projection_visibility_needs_a_derivation_not_only_a_ceiling

## Feature
Document (and where the taxonomy already permits it, enforce) that `GclProjection.visibilityLevel` must be **derived** from `projectionType`, not merely bounded by the workspace's `defaultVisibility` ceiling — closing the gap where a producer's self-declared `visibilityLevel` is never re-examined against what the projected content actually is.
