## Things to flag at Step 2.5
1. **Was `DOMAIN_TABLES`'s loop asserting anything the coverage detector doesn't?** My default vote: **check before deleting.** ⭐ **This is the one place this slice could do harm** — the array is stale, but "stale" and "redundant" are different claims, and only the second licenses deletion. **State what its loop actually asserted.**
