### The mechanism, and it is measured on both sides

⇒ **scrubbing ONE recognized secret unlocks the ENTIRE remaining value under any of 20+ allowlisted fields.** **Forgeable with `AIzaAAAAAAAAAA` — 14 plain ASCII characters, no real credential** (and `sk-aaaaaaaa` already worked before any promotion).
