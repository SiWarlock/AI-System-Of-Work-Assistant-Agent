## ⛔ THE QUESTION, STATED SO IT CANNOT COLLAPSE INTO "ARE THEY THE SAME TYPE?"

For each member: ***must this shape's `replayed` agree with the others, or does it merely happen to match?***
- **Must agree** ⇒ they encode one fact and the duplication is a drift surface (`L88`/`L119` at scale).
- **Merely coincides** ⇒ they are independent facts that share a name, and **unifying them would be a bug**.
⭐ **Both answers are valuable and the second is the one people skip.** A census that returns *"all ten are the
same fact"* without having looked for a counter-example has not measured anything (`L100`).

⛔ **WHY THE CLASS OUTRANKS THE SYMPTOMS, and it bounds any fix you might propose:** `createCommitActivity`
(`commitKnowledge.ts:167-170`) returns a **freshly constructed literal, not a spread** — anything unnamed is
**dropped by construction**, which is exactly where `auditRecord` already dies. ⇒ **adding a discriminator at
any one producer buys nothing while the family drops it**; it would be the **fourth** unconsumed signal here
(`L106`).
