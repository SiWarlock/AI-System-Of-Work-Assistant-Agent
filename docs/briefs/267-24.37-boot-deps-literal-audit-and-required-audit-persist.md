# /tdd brief — boot_deps_literal_audit_and_required_audit_persist (24.37)

## Feature
Close the **class** behind `24.7`'s `briefing`-deps bug: audit every hand-built deps literal in `apps/worker/src/boot.ts` that selectively reuses *some* fields of a factory output, and make `auditPersist` **required at its consumption sites** — not merely complete at construction.

## Two halves, and the second is the one the lead widened this task for

**Half A — the construction audit (the original filing).** `24.7` found `boot.ts`'s `briefing` object (`CopilotBriefingDeps`, feeding `copilotBriefing` — one of three live Copilot entry points) was a hand-built literal reusing `copilot.synthesis` / `workspacePosture` / `routeSelector` **individually** rather than the whole `buildCopilotDeps` output, and did **not** forward the newly-added `auditPersist`. It compiled clean (the field is optional at that layer, deliberately, for test fixtures) but **would have shipped `copilotBriefing`'s denials silently unpersisted.** Fixed in `24.7` — the fix is visible at `boot.ts:2084-2094` — ⭐ **but the pattern is generic, and `briefing` is not guaranteed to be the only instance.**

**Half B — required at consumption (`WIDENED 2026-08-12` by the lead's independent read of `24.7`).** The live deny branches guard the call as `if (deps.auditPersist !== undefined) …` — ⛔ **`L123`'s exact shape: an optional guard is type-indistinguishable from a bound one at the call site**, the same family as `### 24.9`.

⚠ **Not a live defect today** — production always populates it via the now-required `CopilotDepsOptions.auditPersist`, confirmed by the lead's own read. **The exposure is future drift:** nothing at the type level prevents a future `boot.ts` change from silently reintroducing the exact `briefing` gap this same task already fixed once.
